A time machine through music epochs

Kairos Engine

Chronos is the time you travel through. Kairos is the moment you arrive at: when someone's skill, their environment, and what the culture needed converge on one person, once.

Fly from the Baroque courts to the algorithm era and on into four forecast horizons. At every stop, the orrery shows the three forces that had to align, and the dossier names who stood in the beam.

drag to look · ← → thrust · space brake · enter dock · [ ] step epochs · scroll for impulse

Controls

Flying the drive

  • ← → or drag the throttle: thrust toward the past or the future. Release and you coast.
  • space or Brake: thrust against your motion until you stop.
  • enter or Dock: autopilot to the nearest epoch.
  • [ ]: autopilot to the previous or next epoch. Clicking the timeline does the same for any year.
  • Mouse wheel over the view: a velocity impulse. Drag the view to look around.
  • m sound · t theory · esc cancel autopilot or close a panel.

Inside an epoch, select a Kairos case to align the orrery. Beyond 2026 the worlds flicker: that is the forecast uncertainty, rendered.
